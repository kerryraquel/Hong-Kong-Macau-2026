City Impressions / 城市印象

Pearl of the Orient / 東方之珠

A dynamic metropolis where East meets West. Experience the nostalgia of Ding Ding trams, world-class skylines, and authentic local Dai Pai Dongs hidden in the alleys. / 東西文化交融的動感大都會。體驗叮叮車的懷舊風情、世界級的天際線，以及隱藏在巷弄中的地道大排檔。

Must Do / 必做 Victoria Peak, Star Ferry, Tai O / 太平山頂、天星小輪、大澳
Food / 美食 Dim Sum, Milk Tea, Wonton Noodles / 飲茶、奶茶、雲吞麵

Portuguese Heritage / 葡式風情

A perfect blend of European architecture and Chinese culture. Stroll through cobblestone streets, enjoy slow living in Coloane, and experience the dazzling Cotai Strip & Taipa Seaside Promenade. / 歐洲建築與中國文化的完美結合。漫步於鵝卵石街道，在路環享受慢活時光，並體驗璀璨的金光大道與氹仔海濱休憩區。

Must Do / 必做 St. Paul's, Mount Fortress, Taipa Seaside Promenade / 大三巴、大炮台、氹仔海濱休憩區
Food / 美食 Portuguese Tarts, Pork Chop Bun, Almond Dessert / 葡撻、豬扒包、杏仁露
