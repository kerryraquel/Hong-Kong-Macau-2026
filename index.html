City Impressions

Pearl of the Orient

A dynamic metropolis where East meets West. Experience the nostalgia of Ding Ding trams, world-class skylines, and authentic local Dai Pai Dongs hidden in the alleys.

Must Do Victoria Peak, Star Ferry, Tai O
Food Dim Sum, Milk Tea, Wonton Noodles

Portuguese Heritage

A perfect blend of European architecture and Chinese culture. Stroll through cobblestone streets, enjoy slow living in Coloane, and experience the dazzling Cotai Strip & Taipa Seaside Promenade.

Must Do St. Paul's, Mount Fortress, Taipa Seaside Promenade
Food Portuguese Tarts, Pork Chop Bun, Almond Dessert

Neon & Tradition

Tokyo seamlessly intertwines ultra-modern tech with historic shrines. Explore the vibrant youth culture in Harajuku and serene coastal vibes in nearby Kamakura.

Must Do Asakusa, Shimokitazawa, Tokyo Skytree
Food Wagyu Beef, Ramen, Soup Curry
